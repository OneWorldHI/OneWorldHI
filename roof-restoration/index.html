Common questions

Roof restoration, explained.

What is spray-applied roof restoration?

It is a treatment applied across eligible asphalt shingles to help preserve their condition and extend the roof’s useful life.

Is every roof a candidate?

No. Suitability depends on factors such as roof condition, age, shingle integrity, and existing damage. An assessment is required first.

Does restoration repair roof leaks?

Restoration is not a substitute for necessary repairs. Active leaks, structural issues, or significant damage should be identified and addressed separately.

How much longer will my roof last?

Results depend on the roof’s starting condition, environment, materials, and ongoing care. A roof assessment is needed before discussing realistic expectations.

How do I know whether to restore or replace?

A roof that is aging but still structurally sound may be suitable for restoration. Extensive damage or deterioration may make replacement the more appropriate path.
