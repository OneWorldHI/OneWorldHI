Overview

This policy explains the types of information One World Home Improvement may receive through lead forms, phone calls, email communication, and project inquiries, and how that information may be used.

Information you provide

When you request an estimate, consultation, water analysis, or other service, the website may collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your ZIP code
  • Your requested service and project timeline
  • Details you choose to provide about your home or project

How information may be used

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to your request, confirm service availability, discuss project needs, coordinate follow-up, maintain internal records, and improve the website or customer experience.

If you consent to contact, One World Home Improvement may respond by phone, email, or text regarding your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply to text messages depending on your mobile plan.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed using service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, customer relationship management, communications, or related business operations. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect the website, the business, its customers, or others, or when required through an applicable legal process.

Retention and security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures may be used to protect submitted information. No internet transmission or storage method can be assured to be completely secure. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business records, or meet operational obligations.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit a website form. You may also ask the business to update your contact information or stop nonessential follow-up communications by using the confirmed privacy contact information below.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as website features or business practices change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest published version.
